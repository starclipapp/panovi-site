**Conclusion**: What the iPhone Camera’s “Pano” mode captures is a **photo (a still image)**. To keep a wide, moving scene as a **panoramic video**, there are 3 ways: (1) film with the standard Camera’s ultra wide lens (0.5x), (2) use panovi’s “Record with one phone” to record with the wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time, or (3) use panovi to **set two iPhones side by side on a tripod** and stitch them into one video. The one that keeps it widest and in the most detail is (3).

![A frame from a panoramic video shot on two iPhones](/img/hero.jpg "A frame from a panoramic video shot on two iPhones and made into one. The clouds and people are kept in motion")

## How a panorama photo differs from a panoramic video

The iPhone Camera’s “Pano” mode has you move the iPhone sideways while it shoots, and makes one wide photo. It is perfect for still scenery, but it only captures a single moment. People and cars that are moving can come out cut off or warped partway through.

A panoramic video keeps the full wide view **and keeps time too**. Drifting clouds, rolling waves, kids running, the flow of a game. You can look back on a wide scene in motion, just as it happened.

## Comparing the 3 ways

| Method | What you need | Width | Detail | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (1) Standard Camera’s ultra wide (0.5x) | One iPhone | Wide | One iPhone’s worth | When you just want to film wide |
| (2) panovi “Record with one phone” | One iPhone (handheld is fine) | Wide | Sharp wide lens in the center | Quick clips on a walk or a trip |
| (3) panovi with two iPhones | Two iPhones + a tripod | Widest | 4K on each side | Sports days, recitals, stunning views, soccer matches |

## (1) Film with the standard Camera’s ultra wide lens (0.5x)

This is the easiest way. Just choose “0.5” in Video mode in the Camera app. You don’t need to install an app.

But because it takes in a wide view with one iPhone and one lens, distant people and things come out small. If you zoom in afterward, the image tends to look rough.

## (2) panovi’s “Record with one phone”

panovi’s **“Record with one phone”** **records with your iPhone’s wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time** and turns them into one video. The center is footage from the high-resolution wide lens, and around it is footage from the ultra wide lens.

While you shoot, you can zoom just like in the regular Camera app, and the recording keeps everything around the frame. The zoom itself is recorded too, so you can fix how close you get afterward. For details, see “[You can now shoot with one iPhone](post:one-iphone).”

## (3) Set two iPhones side by side on a tripod

This is the basic way to use panovi. Set two iPhones side by side on one tripod, and the left and right footage is stitched into **one ultra-wide panoramic video**.

- **Each side records in 4K**, so you keep more detail than one iPhone filming the same width. When you watch, you can double-tap to zoom in and still see clearly what you want to see
- Once they are on the tripod, you don’t touch them again. **You never have to aim the camera**, so you don’t miss anything
- The second iPhone can be one from family or a friend. Even a different model or a different Apple ID pairs with a QR code

It is made for scenes where you want “everything, edge to edge”: a whole sports day or recital, the view from a mountain summit, an entire soccer match. The steps are explained in “[Getting started with panovi](post:first-video).”

## How it differs from a 360 camera

A 360 camera also films wide, but it spreads its pixels across every direction. That makes each direction rougher, and distant subjects come out only small. panovi uses pixels only in the direction you want to film, so even when you film wide, it keeps things at an impressive size. You don’t need to buy any extra gear either.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a computer or the cloud to make a panoramic video?

No. With panovi, everything from shooting to stitching happens on your iPhone. You don’t need to sign up for an account either. Exporting at 8K size or as 8K + VR (360°) video for posting to YouTube can also be done on your iPhone alone.

### Is it free to use?

Recording, transfer and checking the setup are free for as long as you like. Stitches under 3 minutes are also free forever, with no limit on how many. Only for long stitches of 3 minutes or more, you can try it free 3 times, and after that you pick an event pass (24 hours) or a one-time unlock. It is not a monthly subscription.

### Which iPhones does it work on?

It works on iPhones with iOS 18 or later. When you shoot with two iPhones, they can be different models. “Record with one phone” works on iPhones that have an ultra wide lens and can record with two lenses at the same time.

### Can I film a whole soccer match?

Yes. You can leave it on the tripod with both goals in frame and film the match to the end. Where to set up and how to use the remote are covered in “[Film a whole soccer match](post:whole-match).”
