Views too wide for one iPhone fit edge to edge in a single panovi video. Unlike a photo panorama, **clouds, waves and passing people stay in motion**. Here are 6 tips for using it on your travels.

![From the summit of Mt. Kintoki. Ultra-wide, all the way to Mt. Fuji](/img/hero.jpg "From the summit of Mt. Kintoki. Shot on two iPhones and stitched into one video")

## 1. Include one thing that moves

A still scene can be captured with a photo panorama. If you are shooting video, **include one thing that moves**, like drifting clouds, rolling waves, a passing train or people coming and going. It makes a huge difference to how alive it feels when you watch it again.

20 to 30 seconds in one spot is plenty. **Stitches under 3 minutes are always free, with no limit on how many**, so you can make as many short travel clips as you like without a second thought.

## 2. Keep the tripod level

In ultra-wide footage, even a slight tilt makes the horizon stand out. Once the tripod is set up, first check that it is level. Before recording, use **"Preview 3 seconds of motion"** to check the tilt and the seam together for peace of mind.

## 3. Keep nearby things away from the seam

The seam sits in the middle of the frame. Pick a spot where **things right next to the camera, like an observation deck railing, a handrail or tree branches, don't end up in the middle**, and the stitch comes out clean. See "[How to place them for a clean stitch](post:tripod-setup)" for details.

## 4. No signal? No problem

panovi doesn't use the cloud. Pairing the two iPhones and stitching both happen entirely on the iPhones. **Even on mountaintops or by the sea with no signal, you can shoot and stitch right there.** No account sign-up or login needed.

## 5. Plan for battery and free space

- While recording, use **power save** to turn the screen off and save battery. The recording indicator and elapsed time stay large on screen, so you can tell at a glance that it is recording
- On long shooting days, a portable battery for each iPhone gives you peace of mind
- **"Videos on This Device"** shows your free space at a glance. Test clips that pile up in the app can be deleted one by one while checking their size, or all at once

The second iPhone can belong to family or a friend you are traveling with. Recording and transfer are free, so they only need to install the free app.

## 6. Enjoying it back home

- **Zoom in within the app**: double-tap to zoom in, drag to pan left and right. You can go back later and find signs or people's expressions that were part of the view
- **Publish on YouTube**: with "Export for YouTube", you can make an 8K-size wide video, or an 8K + VR video where viewers can move the view around themselves. No computer needed
- **Zoom in on a big computer screen**: paste a video you uploaded to YouTube, or an mp4 URL, into [panovi viewer](viewer:) to zoom and pan right in your browser
