## About panovi

### What is panovi?

panovi is an iPhone app that turns the left and right footage from two iPhones, set side by side on a tripod, into one **panoramic video (ultra-wide video)**. You capture the whole scene wide, then zoom in on whatever you want to see when you watch. It suits sports days, recitals, travel scenery and soccer matches. There is also “Record with one phone”, which records with one iPhone's wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time.

### Can I shoot a panoramic video on iPhone?

Yes. The iPhone camera's Pano mode makes a still photo, but with panovi you get a panoramic video where people and clouds keep moving. For a detailed comparison of the ways to do it, see “[How do you shoot a panoramic video on iPhone?](post:panorama-video-iphone)”.

### Is panovi the same app as “Panorama Video” on the App Store?

Yes. panovi is a rebuilt new version (2.0) of “Panorama Video” on the App Store. It can't connect to the previous version, so update both iPhones to the latest version before you use it.

### How is panovi different from a 360 camera?

A 360 camera spreads its pixels in every direction, so each direction gets less detail and faraway subjects come out small. panovi puts its pixels only in the direction you want to film, so even when you film wide, subjects stay big and striking. You don't need to buy any special gear, either.

### Does panovi work on Android?

No. panovi is an iPhone app (iOS 18 or later).

## What you need

### How many iPhones do I need?

Two is the standard way to shoot. A third iPhone can be a remote in your hand (optional). If you only have one iPhone, you can use “Record with one phone”. The app also has sample videos built in, so you can see what a two-iPhone stitch looks like first.

### Do the two iPhones have to be the same model?

No. Different models work together. The app automatically picks a lens both can use.

### Do the two iPhones need to use the same Apple ID?

No. Any two iPhones pair just by scanning a QR code. An iPhone borrowed from family or a friend is fine. Recording and transfer are free, so the person you borrow from only needs to install the free app.

### Do I need a tripod, and what should I get?

Yes, when you shoot with two iPhones. We recommend your existing tripod plus a bracket plate for two phones and two MagSafe tripod mounts. “[Placing your iPhones for a clean stitch](post:tripod-setup)” shows how to put it together, with photos.

### Do I need an internet connection?

No. Recording, pairing the two iPhones and stitching all work without the internet. It works just the same on a field or a mountaintop with no signal.

## Shooting

### How long can I record?

There is no limit on recording length. You can record as long as your battery and free space last. Only the length of the video you stitch affects pricing.

### How do I save battery while recording?

Use “power save”, which turns the screen off while recording to save battery. The recording indicator and elapsed time stay large on screen. On days when you film for a long time, plugging a portable battery into both iPhones gives you peace of mind.

### How much free space do I need?

The longer you film, the more you use, because each side records in 4K. If there isn't enough, the app tells you roughly how much you need. Check your free space in “Videos on This Device” and clear out videos you don't need.

### Can I use panovi at a school sports day or a recital?

Yes. Capture everything from edge to edge, then zoom in on your child when you watch. Once it is on the tripod you don't have to aim the camera, so you can focus on what is happening in front of you instead of on filming.

### Can I film a whole soccer match with a fixed camera?

Yes. Set the tripod in line with the halfway line and place two iPhones side by side, and you can film the whole match to the end in one video with both goals in frame. No one has to pan the camera. “[Film a whole soccer match](post:whole-match)” covers how to plan it.

### What can I do with a third iPhone as a remote?

Without touching the cameras, you can start and stop recording, check the seam, check each camera's battery and free space, and pull over the videos you recorded. Just scan the first iPhone's QR code and choose “Use as remote”.

### Can I shoot with just one iPhone?

Yes. “Record with one phone” records with the wide and ultra wide lenses at the same time and turns them into one video. You can zoom while you shoot, and the zoom is recorded too, so you can fix how close you get afterward. It works on iPhones that have an ultra wide lens and can record with two lenses at the same time.

## Stitching, saving and watching

### Do I need a computer to stitch the videos?

No. Everything from recording to stitching, saving and exporting for YouTube happens on the iPhone. You can stitch in the car or on the train home.

### Where is the finished video saved?

Use “Save to Camera Roll” to save it to the Photos app. For a stitched video you saved to the Camera Roll, the copy inside the app is cleared automatically when you close the stitch screen. The video in your Camera Roll is not deleted.

### Can I zoom in on the video afterward?

Yes. Each side records in 4K, so you can double-tap the stitched video to zoom in and drag to pan left and right while you watch.

### Can I upload the video to YouTube?

Yes. “Export for YouTube” turns it into a wide video at 8K size, or an 8K + VR (360°) video where viewers move the view around themselves.

### How do I watch on a big computer screen and zoom in?

Paste the URL of a video you published on YouTube, or of an mp4, into [panovi viewer](viewer:), and you can zoom and pan around inside your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

## Pricing

### Is panovi free?

Mostly, yes. Recording, transfer between devices and the setup check are free for any length, and stitches under 3 minutes are always free, with no limit on how many. The only thing that can cost money is a long stitch of 3 minutes or longer.

### Can I enjoy panovi without paying?

Absolutely. Stitches under 3 minutes are always free, with no limit on how many, so you can make as many short videos as you like: a view on your trip, a moment on a walk, a scene with your kids. Start by shooting lots of short clips and enjoy how wide panovi can go. If you later want to keep a whole game or recital in one long video, that's the time to pick an event pass or the one-time unlock.

### Will I ever be charged unexpectedly?

No. panovi never charges you on its own. You only pay when you choose to buy an event pass or the one-time unlock yourself, and the App Store always asks you to confirm the purchase (with Face ID or similar). There is no subscription and no auto-renewal, so you can use it with peace of mind.

### Can my 3 free stitches get used up without me knowing?

No. When you stitch a video of 3 minutes or longer, the app asks on screen whether to use one of your free stitches. It only uses one when you choose to.

### If a stitch fails, do I lose a free stitch or my event pass?

No. A stitch only counts when the stitched video is saved successfully. If it fails, or stops before it's clear whether it was saved, it doesn't count. Re-stitching the same recording isn't counted twice. The 24 hours of an event pass also start only when the first save succeeds.

### What happens with stitches of 3 minutes or longer?

You can try them 3 times for free. After that, choose between an “event pass (24 hours)” and a “one-time unlock”.

### What is an event pass?

It is a ticket for “just this one day”. From the first stitch you use it on, you get 24 hours of unlimited long stitches. The clock starts when you first use it, not when you buy it, so buying it the day before costs you nothing.

### Is the one-time unlock a subscription?

No. You pay once, and after that it is unlimited forever. There is no monthly or yearly fee.

### Does the owner of a borrowed iPhone have to pay anything?

No. The only thing that can cost money is a stitch of 3 minutes or longer, and even then only on the device of the person doing the stitching.

### What happens to my one-time unlock if I change phones?

You can get it back with “Restore purchase” in “Usage & purchases”. An event pass can't be restored, so buy it close to the day you will use it.

### How much does panovi cost?

Prices differ by country and region, so please check the App Store.

## Privacy

### Is my footage sent anywhere?

No. All processing happens on the iPhone. The app never uploads your videos to the internet.

### Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login either.

## Troubleshooting

### What if the two iPhones won't connect?

Tap “Reset the connection” and scan the QR code again. panovi can't connect to the previous version (Panorama Video 1.x), so also check that both iPhones are updated to the latest version.

### What if the seam looks misaligned?

Bring the two lenses close together at the center and angle them slightly inward so they overlap. It also helps to keep things that pass very close to the camera away from the seam. You can check before you shoot with “Preview 3 seconds of motion”.

### What if I see a band of different brightness in areas like the sky?

Try switching “Matching colour at the seam” on the stitch screen.

### What if the tripod moved partway through recording?

You can split the recording at that point and stitch it. If it still doesn't look right, use “Redo the stitch” to stitch it again a different way.
