05 — Unlike a 360° camera

Spend your pixels
only where you are looking.

A 360° camera spreads its pixels in every direction, so distant subjects stay small. panovi puts every pixel where you are looking, so wide shots stay big.

  • No special gear to buy. Two iPhones you already own, plus a tripod
  • No subscription, no account. The app never uploads your videos to the internet
  • Recording is free and unlimited. Borrow a friend’s iPhone, install the free app, and start shooting right away

09 — FAQ

Things you may wonder.

Can I shoot a panoramic video on iPhone?

Yes. The iPhone camera’s Pano mode makes a still photo, but panovi joins the footage from two iPhones into a panoramic video that keeps moving.

Can I film a whole soccer match with a fixed camera?

Yes. Put the tripod with two iPhones in line with the halfway line, and you get both goals in one video to the final whistle. Free to use, with in-app purchases.

Will I ever be charged unexpectedly?

No. You only pay when you make an in-app purchase yourself, and the App Store always asks you to confirm. No subscription, no auto-renewal.

Is my footage sent anywhere?

No. All processing happens on the iPhones, and no account is needed.
